Privacy policy · effective 17 October 2026

Shaker collects nothing

Shaker has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you make stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

  • Your recipesDrinks, folders, batch recipes and notes are saved only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. They're included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup, which Apple handles.
  • PhotosWhen you import a card picture, Shaker reads only the photos you pick, on your phone, to find the recipe code. Nothing is uploaded.
  • FilesFiles you choose to import are read on your phone. Files you save or share go only where you send them with the iOS share sheet.
  • SharingA share code or card picture contains the recipes you chose to share, and nothing else. Your notes are never included.
  • PurchasesFolder purchases go through Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple ID. The app keeps a note of what you've bought on your phone.
  • ChildrenShaker is for adults. It doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions about privacy: stamhadsoftware@gmail.com

Support

Help with Shaker

Questions, problems or ideas? Email us and we'll get back to you. stamhadsoftware@gmail.com

How do I share a drink or a folder?

Open a drink and tap the share button for a card picture or a share code. For a folder, open it and tap Share, then Copy share code or Save as file. Folder sharing includes batch recipes.

How do I import what someone sent me?

Tap + then Import. Choose photos for card pictures, Choose file for a Shaker file or backup, or paste the share message. If a drink has the same name as one of yours, Shaker shows the differences and asks whether to keep yours, replace it or keep both.

How do I move my drinks to a new iPhone?

  1. On the old phone, tap + then Back up my drinks, and save the file to Files or AirDrop it.
  2. On the new phone, tap + then Import, then Choose file, and pick the backup.

Restoring your iPhone from an iCloud backup also brings your drinks back.

How do batches work?

Open a drink and tap Batches. Shaker drafts a bottle recipe from the single recipe and works out how many drinks it makes and how much to pour per drink. Fresh juice, egg and top-ups can be marked as added to each glass instead of the bottle.

Folders and purchases

One folder free, more when you want them

Drinks are always free and unlimited. Your first folder is free. Extra folders are one-time purchases, or you can unlock unlimited folders once.

I bought folders. How do I get them on another phone?

Tap + then New folder, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought them with.

I have a code

Tap + then New folder, then Redeem code, and enter it. You can also open the code's link on your iPhone.

Refunds

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
